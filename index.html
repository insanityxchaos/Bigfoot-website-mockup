Do I need a medical card?

Yes. Oklahoma only allows dispensary sales to patients and caregivers with a valid OMMA license. You can apply online with the Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority. Out-of-state patients can apply for a temporary license.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. Walk in or pick up an order any time, day or night, seven days a week.

How much can I buy?

Oklahoma patients can carry up to 3 ounces of flower, 1 ounce of concentrate and 72 ounces of edibles. Our register checks your limits at checkout.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Cannabis is still illegal under federal law, so card networks don't process credit purchases. We take cash and debit.

How long do you hold a reservation?

We hold pickup orders for 24 hours. After that the items go back on the shelf.

Can I return a product?

State rules don't allow returns of opened cannabis products. If a cartridge or device is defective, bring it back within 7 days with your receipt and we'll exchange it.

Can I consume on site?

No. State law prohibits consumption at the store and in public places, including the parking lot.
